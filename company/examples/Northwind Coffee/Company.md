# Northwind Coffee

> Fictional worked example. This is the durable company picture. Live campaign state and open tasks belong in the operating system.

**Updated:** 2026-06-18

## What it is

Northwind Coffee is an independent wholesale coffee roaster. It supplies roasted coffee, equipment guidance, and account support to cafes and offices in its local region.

The company also has a small direct-to-consumer range, but wholesale accounts are the operating priority.

## Business model

Wholesale customers buy roasted coffee on recurring supply arrangements. A typical cafe account is worth $1,200 to $2,800 per month, depending on volume and the blend selected. Office accounts are smaller and usually buy on a fortnightly schedule.

The company produced $1.8 million in revenue last year. Around 78% came from wholesale cafes and offices. The remaining revenue came from online and roastery-door sales.

## Who it serves

- Independent cafes that need reliable supply, practical support, and consistent coffee
- Multi-site hospitality groups that want one roaster across several venues
- Offices that want a simple recurring coffee service without managing retail orders

Most customers are within the local delivery region. Northwind does not position itself as a national supplier.

## Current state

Wholesale retention is strong, but new-account growth has slowed. The team is preparing a Q3 wholesale campaign while tightening the handoff between marketing enquiries and operations.

The campaign concept is approved. The next major output is a three-email sequence for prospective wholesale accounts.

## Who is involved

- Tom Reilly, founder. Owns brand direction and approves material budget decisions.
- Priya Shah, operations manager. Owns wholesale delivery, account onboarding, and the day-to-day campaign handoff.

Priya has her own file in `brain/People/` because she is the main contact for current wholesale work.

## What the company is trying to make happen

Northwind wants a repeatable flow of qualified wholesale enquiries without sacrificing service quality for existing accounts. The near-term goal is to make the Q3 campaign useful, then connect it to a clear operational follow-up process.

## What is in the way

The company does not have a consistent handoff from a new enquiry to a qualified sales conversation. Marketing can generate interest, but ownership becomes unclear once an enquiry needs pricing, equipment detail, or a delivery check.

## Key numbers

- $1.8 million revenue last year
- 78% of revenue from wholesale accounts
- Typical cafe account value of $1,200 to $2,800 per month
- 42 active wholesale accounts
- 91% wholesale account retention over the last 12 months

## Working rules

- Give Priya one recommendation with the reasoning, not a long menu of options.
- Involve Tom for budget or brand-direction decisions.
- Treat local delivery capacity as a real constraint when assessing campaign ideas.
- Keep direct-to-consumer work separate from the current wholesale campaign.
