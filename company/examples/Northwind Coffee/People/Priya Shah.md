# Priya Shah

> Fictional worked example of a People file.

**Role:** Operations manager
**Where:** Northwind Coffee
**Updated:** 2026-06-18

## Who they are

Priya runs wholesale operations, including delivery planning, account onboarding, and the handoff from a new enquiry to an active account. She is the main contact for the Q3 wholesale campaign.

## How to deal with them

Priya decides quickly when she has a clear recommendation and the operational consequence. Lead with the answer, then give the short reason. Do not send a list of equal options and ask her to choose without a view.

Keep updates practical. State what changed, what she needs to decide, and what happens next.

## What they care about

- Reliable service for the 42 active wholesale accounts
- New business that fits local roasting and delivery capacity
- Clear ownership when a marketing enquiry needs pricing or an operational check
- Avoiding campaign promises the delivery team cannot support

## History and context

Priya approved the Q3 wholesale campaign concept on 18 June 2026. She asked for the final three-email sequence before the campaign build moves into production.

She is updating the wholesale price list. The sequence should not quote prices until she confirms the new version.
