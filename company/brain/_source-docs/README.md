# Source Docs

Dump your raw documents here. Pitch decks, reports, old strategy notes, exports, transcripts, anything relevant that is lying around. Do not organise it. This is your raw material, your mine, not your brain.

The brain comes next. Your AI inventories this folder, groups what it finds by business and person, and refines the signal into clean files: `brain/Company.md` per business, `brain/People/<name>.md` per key person. Run `prompts/mine-your-docs.md` to do this.

## Two rules

1. **Your originals stay untouched.** The AI reads from here and writes clean files elsewhere. It never edits or deletes what you drop in. This folder is reference, the brain is the product.

2. **The AI ignores this folder on normal runs.** It is archive, not brain. Your `CLAUDE.md` tells the AI to skip `_source-docs/` unless you explicitly ask it to mine the dump. This keeps everyday answers fast and on-signal.

## How to think about it

A folder of dumped documents is a storage unit, not a brain. The win is not having everything in one place. The win is refining the signal out of it into a few clean files the AI reads on every run. Build one or two businesses deep before you do all of them. Depth on what matters now beats breadth across everything.
