### How to use

- Log it the moment you hand something off or ask for something. "Sent the contract to legal, waiting on redlines."
- `(asked: date)` and `(chase after: date)` let the sweep tell you when a follow-up is overdue without you tracking it.
- The sweep checks this against your inbox: if the thing you were waiting for arrived, your AI moves it to "Recently cleared" and may surface a task.
- A short, honest waiting-for list is one of the highest-leverage files you keep. Most dropped balls are things sitting in someone else's court that you forgot to chase.
