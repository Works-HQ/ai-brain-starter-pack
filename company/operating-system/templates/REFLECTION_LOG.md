### Why reflect, not just debrief

Debrief processes the work. Reflection improves the *machine that does the work*. Every time your AI gives you a generic answer, that is a missing file. Every time a sweep wastes your time, that is a tuning fix. Reflection is where you catch those and fix them, so the system gets sharper instead of drifting. Ten minutes a week. It is the difference between a system that compounds and one that quietly goes stale.
