### Why this file earns its place

Most decisions get made in a meeting or a head, and the *why* evaporates. Six weeks later someone reopens it, or your AI proposes the thing you already killed. Writing the why down once saves the argument every time after. When you run a debrief, decisions get filed here automatically. When you ask your AI for options on this project, it reads this first and will not re-pitch what you have rejected.
