### How to use

- The `weekly-update` skill generates each new section from the week's meeting notes, tasks, decisions, and waiting-for items. You review and correct, you do not write it from scratch.
- "Status" is a deliberate forcing function. Green/Amber/Red makes you triage at a glance.
- One update per project or business unit. Run them all on the same day and you get a portfolio view.
- Keep it compact. If a weekly update runs longer than a screen, it is a strategy doc, not an update. Move the deep thinking elsewhere.
