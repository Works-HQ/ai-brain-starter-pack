# About me

> Worked example. This is a fictional persona so you can see what "good" looks
> like. Yours will be shorter at first and grow as you use it.

## Who I am
- Name: Jordan Lee
- Role / what I do: Freelance brand and marketing consultant
- Day to day: I run marketing for three retainer clients at once, mostly strategy,
  campaign planning, and writing. I'm a team of one, so my time is the constraint.

## What I work across
- Northwind Coffee: a wholesale coffee roaster, I run their B2B marketing
- Atlas Fitness: a boutique gym chain, I handle launch campaigns for new sites
- My own practice: inbound, proposals, and keeping the pipeline warm

## How I work
- How I like things done: get to the point, give me a draft I can react to, not a
  list of options to choose from
- What good output looks like: specific, uses the client's real context, ready to
  send with light edits
- What I can't stand: generic filler, being asked questions I've already answered

## My voice
- Warm but direct. Short sentences. Plain words, no marketing jargon.
- Hard rules: no exclamation marks, never open with "I hope this finds you well".

## People in my orbit
- Priya (Northwind): my main contact, the ops manager, decides fast
- Marcus (Atlas): founder, busy, prefers a one-line summary then the detail

## What I mainly want help with
- Drafting campaign plans and client emails in my voice
- Keeping each client's status straight so I never walk into a call cold
- Turning messy call notes into filed tasks and decisions
